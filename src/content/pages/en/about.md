# About

Hi, I'm **Aryan**. I'm a programmer who's **in love with modern and cutting-edge technology!**
In 2022, I got my first job in programming as a **.Net Back-End Developer**.
Since then, I've worked with many tools and on various projects, but my main focus has mostly been **.NET**.


Early in my career, I loved **over-engineering** things and using new stuff in my projects, but recently I've become a fan of **simplicity**.
You can see my interest in simplicity throughout this blog too.
*Johan Cruyff* (the Dutch football legend, creator of Total Football and Tiki-Taka) has a famous quote:
> *Playing football is simple, but playing simple football is the hardest thing possible*

I think this quote is also true for **software development**.


I know a bit about infrastructure and **DevOps** concepts, and I'm very interested in them. I try to study them in my free time.

I'm a huge fan of **AI** and try not to fall behind its trends (even though it's really hard), and I continuously study it.

I've tried frontend several times, but the output was never satisfactory :) But I'm sure **one day I'll master that too :)**


I completed my studies in *Computer Science* at *Islamic Azad University-Science and Research Branch*.

But well, *I learned everything I know outside of university :)*


## About This Blog

In this blog, I'm going to **share** the things I'm learning myself. The experiences I've gained, the right and wrong paths I've taken, and…

There won't be any **zero-to-hero tutorials** here; there are much more experienced people and much more credible resources for that.

I'll try to explain things in **simple and conversational language**. In my opinion, someone who talks in a complicated and overly fancy way isn't cool; **being cool means explaining something difficult in a way that everyone can understand.** That's what I'm trying to do: keep things simple.


**Things you might see on this blog:**

* A deep dive into **database indexes**
* What is **Cloudflare** and what services does it offer?
* How to properly use **AI** in the development environment
* My opinion on a particular topic
* The differences between **C#** and **Go**
* How to write a **good resume** and how to **apply**


**Things you won't see:**

* ~~Project-based C# tutorials~~
* ~~Clean code tutorials~~
* ~~Those LinkedIn posts that say "don't write code like that, write it like this" and get 50 reposts~~
* ~~click-baite titles~~
* ~~Is programming dead in 2093?~~
